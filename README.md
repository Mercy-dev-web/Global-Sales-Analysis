# Global Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores the sales performance of a global retail business using Microsoft Excel.

The analysis covers 3,000 sales transactions recorded between 2024 and 2025 across different countries, regions, product categories, sales channels, and customer segments.

I used Excel to analyse the data and build an interactive dashboard that gives a clearer picture of the business's performance. The goal was to understand what was driving sales, which products and categories were performing well, and where the business could improve.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel formulas and calculated fields
- Data cleaning and analysis
- Interactive dashboard features

## Areas of Analysis

The dashboard is organised into four main sections:

**1. Sales Performance**
- Total revenue and profit
- Sales performance across regions
- Revenue by country
- Sales channel performance
- Sales trends over time

**2. Product Performance**
- Revenue by product category
- Best-performing products
- Units sold across products and subcategories
- Profit margin across product categories
- Product return rates

**3. Customer Demographics**
- Revenue across customer segments
- Customer distribution
- Customer satisfaction
- Performance across different countries and regions

**4. Customer Analysis**
- Total orders
- Average order value
- Customer satisfaction
- Return rate
- Shipping performance

## Key Findings

After analysing the data, I identified a few findings worth paying attention to:

- **Electronics generated the highest revenue**, contributing approximately 1.33 million to total sales. However, it also recorded the highest return rate among the product categories.
- **Mountain Bike X5 was the highest-revenue product**, generating approximately 279,987 in revenue. It also recorded a strong profit margin of about 40%.
- **Online sales generated the highest revenue** among the four sales channels, with approximately 1.37 million.
- **North America recorded the highest revenue by region**, contributing approximately 838,854.
- **Argentina was the highest-revenue country**, with approximately 309,349 in sales.
- **Consumer customers contributed the highest revenue**, while Small Business customers also made a significant contribution.
- **Wholesale had the highest return rate among the sales channels**, suggesting that the business may need to pay closer attention to returned orders from this channel.

## Business Recommendations

Based on these findings, I would recommend the following actions:

**1. Investigate product returns within Electronics**

Electronics generated the highest revenue, but it also had the highest return rate. The business should investigate the products responsible for these returns, review customer complaints, and check whether product descriptions, quality, or delivery issues are contributing to the problem. Reducing avoidable returns could help protect profit.

**2. Give more attention to high-performing products**

Mountain Bike X5 and Bike Helmet Aero generated the highest product revenues. The business should monitor demand for these products, maintain sufficient stock, and consider targeted promotions or product bundles. This could help the company build on products that are already performing well.

**3. Strengthen the online sales channel**

Online sales contributed the largest share of revenue. The business should continue improving the online shopping experience, paying attention to product presentation, checkout processes, delivery, and customer support. It should also monitor whether these improvements lead to more sales and repeat purchases.

**4. Review the wholesale return rate**

Wholesale recorded the highest return rate across the sales channels. I would recommend reviewing wholesale orders to understand why products are being returned. The business could also improve order verification and communication with wholesale customers before orders are dispatched.

**5. Build on customer segment performance**

Consumer customers contributed the most revenue, while Small Business customers also accounted for a considerable share. The business could develop targeted offers for both groups instead of using the same marketing approach for every customer segment.

## Conclusion

This project helped me explore how sales data can be used to understand business performance beyond just looking at total revenue.

By analysing products, regions, sales channels, and customer segments, I was able to identify areas performing well and others that may need attention.

The dashboard brings these findings together in one place, making it easier to monitor performance and identify opportunities for improvement.
